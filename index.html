Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about how we work. Need something else? Use the contact form and we will get back to you.

Do you work on both residential and commercial properties?

Yes. Every service we offer is available for homes and for commercial spaces such as offices, shops and rental properties.

Which services do you offer?

We provide home improvement services, plumbing installation, electrical repair and painting. Many projects combine more than one of these.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the contact form with a short description of the job, or call us. We will confirm the scope with you and give you a quote before any work begins.

Can you handle several services in one project?

Yes. For example, we can complete repairs or new installations and then paint the finished space, so you deal with one team from start to finish.

Will you protect my property while you work?

Yes. We cover floors, furniture and fixtures, and we clean the work area when the job is complete.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the size and type of work. We give you an estimated schedule together with your quote, and we tell you early if anything changes.
